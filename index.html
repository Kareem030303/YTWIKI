Most Popular Video

"Baby Shark Dance" — Pinkfong

Released in 2016, this children's music video grew into a massive global phenomenon and holds the record as the most-watched video in YouTube history.

First Video on YouTube

"Me at the zoo" — Jawed Karim

Uploaded on April 23, 2005, this is the very first video ever uploaded to YouTube, featuring co-founder Jawed Karim at the San Diego Zoo.
